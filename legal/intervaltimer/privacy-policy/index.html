Effective date: 2026-07-31 — updated on every material change.

This page describes what the Interval Timer app does and does not do with your data. We've tried to keep it short and specific instead of long and legalistic — if something on this page is unclear, email the address above and we'll fix it.

TL;DR

  • We don't ask for an account. We don't have a server. We don't profile you.
  • Your workouts, presets, and settings live only on your device.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob (a third party) and appear only on the workout-complete screen. AdMob may use a non-identifying advertising ID to choose an ad. You can reset or limit that ID in your device's Settings → Privacy at any time.
  • The one-time "Pro" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing; we never see your card or payment details.
  • The app uses Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports — no personal data. You can switch it off in Settings → General → "Share anonymous usage data."
  • The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personally identifying information. Period.

What stays on your device

Everything that makes the app "yours" stays in the app's local storage, never leaves the device, and never reaches us:

  • Saved workout presets (Tabata, HIIT, Pomodoro, boxing rounds, custom circuits, and any presets you create).
  • Your settings — theme, language, sound and voice-cue toggles, vibration intensity, accessibility preferences.
  • Your Pro entitlement (whether you've purchased the upgrade). We store only the fact of the purchase, never the underlying receipt or any Google account identifier.

You can erase all of this at any time by uninstalling the app or by clearing the app's data in your device's Settings → Apps → Interval Timer.

What third parties may collect (and how to control it)

Google AdMob (advertising)

When ads are enabled, AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID (AAID) on your device, plus coarse device and network metadata (model, OS version, country, language, IP-derived country) to pick an ad. AdMob's behavior is governed by Google's Privacy & Terms and their Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

You can opt out of personalized ads or reset the AAID at any time:

  • Android 13+: Settings → Security & Privacy → Privacy → Ads.
  • Android 12 and below: Settings → Google → Ads.

Ads appear only on the workout-complete screen — never during an active workout. Buying the in-app "Pro" upgrade removes all ads entirely.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchase)

The "Pro" purchase is processed by Google Play. Google Play handles your payment and gives the app a confirmation that you own the item. We never receive or store your payment method, billing address, or Google account.

Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (anonymous usage and crash reporting)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate and to detect and fix crashes. This collection is anonymous: it is not tied to your name, email, or any account, and we do not use it to identify or profile you.

  • Analytics records non-identifying events — such as starting or completing a workout, saving or duplicating a preset, being shown the "Pro" upsell, unlocking Pro, and finishing onboarding — along with coarse device and app metadata (app version, OS version, device model, language, and an IP-derived country; we do not store the IP address itself). Firebase assigns a resettable app-instance ID.
  • Crashlytics records crash and error reports — a stack trace plus device state at the moment of the crash and an anonymous installation identifier — so we can reproduce and fix problems.

Firebase's handling of this data is governed by Firebase's Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy & Terms.

You can turn all of this off at any time in the app: Settings → General → "Share anonymous usage data." It is on by default; when it is off, no analytics events or crash reports leave your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. AdMob's content rating is configured to "General audiences." If you believe a child has used the app on your device, you can uninstall it to clear all locally stored progress.

Permissions the app requests

  • Internet — to load ads, verify the in-app purchase, and (unless you opt out) send anonymous usage analytics and crash reports. The timer is fully functional offline; only these features need a network.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — to keep the timer running and fire audio/vibration cues while the screen is off, the app is backgrounded, or the device is locked.
  • Notifications — to show the persistent timer notification with lock-screen controls during a workout.
  • Vibrate — to provide haptic feedback on phase transitions.
  • Wake lock — to keep the screen on during a workout when the keep-screen-on option is enabled.
  • (No other runtime permissions.) The app does not request contacts, photos, microphone, location, calendar, or storage.
